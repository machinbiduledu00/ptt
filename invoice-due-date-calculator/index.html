How invoice due dates work

An invoice due date is the date by which payment is expected under the agreed payment terms. Net 30 means payment is due 30 calendar days after the invoice date.

Standard Net terms normally refer to calendar days unless your agreement says otherwise. Showing the actual due date on an invoice helps avoid ambiguity for both parties.

Frequently asked questions

What is an invoice due date?

It is the date by which a customer is expected to pay an invoice under the agreed payment terms.

How do I calculate a Net 30 due date?

Add 30 calendar days to the invoice date. The invoice date itself is day 0.

Does Net 30 mean 30 calendar days?

Usually, yes. If an agreement requires business days or another convention, follow that agreement instead.

Can I calculate Net 60 or Net 90?

Yes. Select Net 60 or Net 90 from the payment terms list.

Can I use a custom payment term?

Yes. Select Custom and enter any positive whole number of calendar days.

Does the calculator account for weekends or holidays?

No. It uses normal calendar-day arithmetic and does not move dates that fall on weekends or holidays.
